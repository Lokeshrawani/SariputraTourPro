An all-in-one travel marketplace platform tailored for pilgrimage packages, local Bihar tours (Bodh Gaya, Rajgir, Nalanda), and pan-India vehicle rentals. Featuring instant WhatsApp enquiries, direct UPI payment integration, and a browser-based AI travel assistant.

---

## 🌟 Features

* **🛒 E-Commerce UX:** Flipkart-inspired search, filter buttons, and dynamic tour package cards.
* **🚐 Fleet Booking Desk:** Rentals ranging from 7-seater family cars and 17-seater Tempo Travellers / Force Urbanias to 49-seater tourist coaches.
* **📲 Instant WhatsApp Enquiry:** Auto-generates structured travel booking quotes sent directly to WhatsApp.
* **💳 Direct Payment Integration:** Deep-linked UPI payment launcher for instant advance token bookings.
* **🤖 Voice-Enabled AI Assistant:** In-browser AI assistant with Speech-to-Text (Voice input) and Text-to-Speech (Voice answers).
* **🔌 Dual Backend Architecture:** Starter APIs available in both **Python Flask** and **Java Spring Boot**.
